Lead From Your Values. Build Power in Community.

A leadership program for emerging leaders ready to strengthen their voice, lead with authenticity, and create change alongside others.

  • Cost: Free — at no cost to participants
  • Format: Hybrid
  • Stipend: $800
  • Commitments: Workshops twice a month, monthly mentoring sessions, and an in-person gathering
  • Eligibility: Ages 18–25. Emerging leaders who collaborate with, organize, or mobilize others; have lived experience navigating systemic barriers; and are committed to learning and leading with purpose and integrity.

What You'll Learn

Values-Based Leadership

Participants identify their personal values, challenge traditional leadership myths, explore different leadership styles, and develop principles for authentic, purpose-driven leadership.

Healing, Power, and Resilience

Participants explore healing as a leadership practice, develop grounding strategies, examine power and privilege, and learn to lead with awareness, confidence, and accountability.

Personal and Collective Storytelling

Participants learn to communicate their lived experiences with intention, establish appropriate boundaries, and connect individual stories to a shared purpose.

What You'll Gain During the Program

  • Greater clarity about personal values and leadership style
  • Tools for navigating self-doubt and building confidence
  • Grounding practices that support clarity and resilience
  • A stronger understanding of power, privilege, and inclusive leadership
  • Storytelling skills for communicating lived experiences
  • Experience building trust, safety, and accountability within a group
  • Connection and community through shared reflection

Opportunities After Completion

  • An alumni network
  • Internships or employment opportunities
  • Volunteer or community-leadership roles
  • Continued workshops or advanced programming
  • Mentorship
  • Speaking or facilitation opportunities
  • References
  • Opportunities within Alianza for Opportunity
